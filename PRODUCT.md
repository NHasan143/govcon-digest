## Platform

web

## Users

GovCon professionals choosing coverage areas across government contracting, defense, federal technology, and related business news. Confirmed by the user for the Topics redesign.

Editors and administrators use the private CMS sign-in and dashboard to create, resume, and manage publication content. The user confirmed both surfaces are in scope for the CMS redesign.

## Product Purpose

An editorial publication for browsing reporting by section and topic. The Topics directory helps readers choose a coverage area quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Seven sections, each with three subsections, are defined in `lib/categories.ts`. Preserve all existing category names, descriptions, and flat route destinations. Category hubs aggregate section and subsection posts. Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, and Payload CMS underpin the existing application.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing newspaper style, as confirmed by the user. Preserve the incumbent logo and publication identity. Site identity comes from `lib/config.ts`.

## Evidence on Hand

The category registry supplies approved names, descriptions, and section colors. Existing page code and assets establish the publication's current presentation. Do not invent news, statistics, or commercial claims.
